01 / About

From first principles to portfolio risk

Models regulators trust, and decisions stakeholders can act on.

I began in physics at IISER Bhopal — one of India's most selective science and research institutes — where I learned to reason about uncertainty from first principles. The same question, what is the true distribution of outcomes?, is what drew me into quantitative credit risk.

Today I develop, validate and monitor the models that measure credit losses and regulatory capital: PD, LGD and EAD estimation, scorecards, loss and revenue forecasting, and stress testing across retail and wholesale portfolios.

I work to a governance standard — transparent assumptions, defensible documentation, and challenger testing rather than black boxes — and I've presented model design and findings directly to Model Risk Management. I'm equally at home in a SAS validation pack and a Python forecasting pipeline.

Always glad to connect with teams working on credit-risk modeling, model validation or decision science in financial services.

02 / Expertise

What I work on

Model development & forecasting

PD, LGD and EAD estimation, application and behavioural scorecards, PPNR and loss forecasting, and flow-rate PD term structures — across retail and wholesale portfolios.

Independent model validation

End-to-end validation of scorecards and capital models: discrimination, calibration and stability testing, reject inference, benchmarking and scenario stress testing — aligned to SR 11-7.

Regulatory monitoring & governance

Ongoing performance monitoring under IFRS 9, CECL and CCAR: accuracy and stability indicators, RAG reporting, loss forecasts, and findings prepared for model-risk governance.

Methods & tooling

Regression, time-series, PCA and gradient-boosted models with explainability — delivered in Python, SAS and SQL.
